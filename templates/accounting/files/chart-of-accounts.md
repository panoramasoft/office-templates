# Chart of accounts

The categories the books use. Edit this before the first goal: keep the ones you need, add your own. The team never invents a category; anything that doesn't fit is marked for you to decide.

## Money in
- Sales
- Services
- Interest
- Other income

## Money out
- Cost of goods
- Rent
- Utilities
- Software and subscriptions
- Office supplies
- Travel
- Meals
- Professional fees (accountant, lawyer)
- Bank fees
- Taxes paid
- Salaries and contractors
- Other expenses

## Not income or expense
- Transfer between own accounts
- Owner's money in
- Owner's drawings
- Loan received / loan repaid
